## 📐 3D Models (STL)

The 3D models used in the robotic chess arm are provided in **STL format** and are organized into two main folders:

```text
stl/
├── Arm/
└── Claw/
```

* **`Arm/`** — Contains the 3D models corresponding to the structure and mechanical components of the robotic arm.
* **`Claw/`** — Contains the 3D models corresponding to the robotic claw and its gripping mechanism.

The STL files can be used for **3D printing, visualization, and mechanical reference** of the robotic system.
